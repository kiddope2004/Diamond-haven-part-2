## website-Goals and objectives
The goal of the Diamond Haven website is to showcase our luxury diamond rings, necklaces, bracelets, and watches with elegance and trust. We aim to build brand credibility, educate customers on ethical diamonds, and drive high-value sales and private consultations. The site should create a seamless, exclusive online experience that turns visitors into lifelong clients — both in-store and online.
	Specific website goals:
 To turn website visitors into appointments, and appointments into lifelong Diamond Haven clients.
